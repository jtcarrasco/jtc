You've probably heard a lot about AI lately. Maybe way too much. Most of it is aimed at developers and tech companies, which doesn't help much if you run a landscaping business or a dental office. So here's the practical version: three tools you can actually use this week, and what each one is good for.

## 1. ChatGPT: your first-draft machine

ChatGPT is made by OpenAI, and it's the one most people have heard of. Think of it as a first-draft machine.

Stuck replying to a tricky client email? Describe the situation to ChatGPT and ask it to draft a professional reply. You'll still edit it, but getting past the blank page is half the battle. Same goes for social media posts, service descriptions, FAQ pages, job listings, or really any writing you'd otherwise put off until next week... or next month.

**What it's good for:** Writing, summarizing, answering questions, brainstorming.

**Free tier:** Yes. The free version runs on an older model, but it handles most everyday business tasks just fine.

**Paid plan:** $20/month (ChatGPT Plus) gets you GPT-5, OpenAI's newest and most capable model. It's noticeably better at careful writing and complicated questions.

**Get started:** [chatgpt.com](https://chatgpt.com)

## 2. Claude: for longer, more complex work

Claude is made by Anthropic, and it's especially good with long documents. If you need to paste in a contract, a report or a long email thread and ask questions about it, this is the one I'd reach for.

It's also a bit more careful than some of the others. It's less likely to make things up and more likely to say "I'm not sure" when it isn't. When accuracy matters for your business, that's worth a lot.

**What it's good for:** Reading and summarizing long documents, writing that needs a consistent tone, research help.

**Free tier:** Yes. It covers most casual use.

**Paid plan:** $20/month (Claude Pro), or a little less if you pay for the year up front. If you know you'll use it regularly, the annual plan saves you a few bucks.

**Get started:** [claude.ai](https://claude.ai)

## 3. Google Gemini: already in your Google account

If your business runs on Gmail or Google Workspace, you already have Gemini. It's built right into Gmail, Google Docs, Google Drive and Google Sheets, so you don't have to copy and paste between apps.

You can ask Gemini to summarize an email thread, help you draft a reply, pull information out of files in your Drive, write formulas in Sheets, or take notes on a Google Meet call. It's not always the most powerful of the three, but having it inside the tools you already use every day is a real convenience, and it keeps getting better.

**What it's good for:** Working inside Gmail, Google Docs, Sheets and Meet without switching tabs.

**Free tier:** Available in personal Google accounts. Google Workspace plans come with extra Gemini features.

**Get started:** It's already in your Google account. Look for the Gemini icon in Gmail or Docs.

---

## Where to start

Pick one and try it on one real task this week. Write that email you've been dodging, or ask it to describe your services three different ways and see which one sounds the most like you.

The biggest mistake people make with AI is expecting it to be perfect on the first try. It won't be. Think of it as an assistant, not a replacement. It's a starting point, and a pretty good one once you get the hang of asking it for things. You stay in charge, and it handles the busywork.
