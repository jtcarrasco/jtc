_Last updated: October 17, 2026_

Short version: this site collects very little, doesn't use tracking cookies, and doesn't sell anything about you to anyone. Here's the longer version.

## Analytics

I use [Umami](https://umami.is), a privacy-focused analytics tool that runs on my own server. It counts page views, referring sites, browser and device type, and rough country. It doesn't use cookies, doesn't track you across other sites, and doesn't store your IP address. I use it to see which posts people actually read.

## Contact form

If you send a message through the [contact page](/contact/), the form is handled by [Formspree](https://formspree.io), which passes your name, email and message on to me. I use that only to reply to you. I don't add you to a mailing list. Formspree's own [privacy policy](https://formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy) covers how they handle it in transit.

## Affiliate links

Some links on the [Uses](/uses/) and [Bookshelf](/bookshelf/) pages are Amazon affiliate links. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases, at no extra cost to you. If you click one, Amazon may set cookies in your browser to track the referral. That happens on Amazon's side, under [Amazon's privacy notice](https://www.amazon.com/privacy).

## Third-party services

A few things on the site load from other companies, which means your browser connects to them and they can see your IP address:

- **Hosting:** the site is hosted on GitHub Pages and served through Cloudflare. Both keep standard server logs (IP address, browser, pages requested) for security and performance.
- **Fonts:** fonts load from Google Fonts.
- **Embedded media:** some blog posts embed YouTube or Vimeo videos or GitHub Gists. Those players can set their own cookies once they load.

## Your data

I don't sell or share personal information. If you've contacted me and want me to delete your messages, use the [contact form](/contact/) and I'll take care of it.

## Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
