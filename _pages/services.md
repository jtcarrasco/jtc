## Tech Training & Consulting for Individuals and Small Businesses

Most of the individuals and small business owners I work with are great at what they do — the tech side is the part that slows them down. I offer one-on-one, hands-on help with the specific tools, problems, and workflows that are actually in your way. No generic courses, no overwhelming manuals. Available in-person throughout the Beach Cities and South Bay Los Angeles area, or remotely wherever you are.

---

### AI Tools for Your Business (or Your Life)
Most people have heard about AI but haven't found a way to make it actually useful. I'll show you how to use ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini for the specific tasks in front of you — writing, research, customer communications, content, internal processes, or just everyday personal use. We work with your real workflow, not hypothetical examples.

### Cloud and Device Setup
Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, iCloud, shared drives and calendars, email on every device. Getting everything connected and working the way it should.

### Cybersecurity Basics
Passwords, two-factor authentication, phishing awareness, account security, and backups. Most small businesses and individuals are one compromised password away from a bad day. We fix that.

### Automation and Workflow
Connecting the software you already use so it works together — Zapier, Make.com, or just showing you the features you're paying for but not using.

### Software and Tools Training
Canva, Google Analytics, Meta Business Suite, Mailchimp — hands-on training for whatever tools your business or daily life runs on.

### General Tech Help and Troubleshooting
Devices, printers, networks, slow computers, "it was working yesterday." In-person for hardware issues, remote for everything else.
